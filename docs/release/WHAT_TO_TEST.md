Writing controls have moved to a row under the top bar, where a hand does not
rest on them. Pen, pencil, highlighter, eraser, lasso and shapes, three widths
for the tool you are using, a row of colours and saved favourites are all one
tap. Favourites carry the tool, colour and width together, so a fine black pen
and a yellow highlighter are one tap apart; long-press a favourite, or use
More ▸ Favourites, to edit and reorder them. Your existing pens and colours were
carried over, not replaced.

Three saving and undo bugs are fixed. A stroke could be written to the file in
an out-of-date form, and — worse — could leave the page looking saved so that
everything drawn after it was never written at all. Undo immediately after a
stroke did nothing until a timer happened to fire. And a page whose handwriting
could not be read was shown as blank and then let you write on it, which
overwrote the original; it now says so and offers to try again.

New, both optional:

• Scribble to erase — cross handwriting out with the pen or pencil to remove it,
  without switching tools. OFF by default: Settings ▸ Writing gestures. Please
  try to make it erase something it shouldn't — a sine wave, shading, hatching,
  repeated letters, a big Σ. One undo puts the writing back.

• Draw and hold — draw a rough line, circle or rectangle and hold the pen still
  at the end; a clean one appears, dragging resizes it, lifting keeps it. ON by
  default. A deliberate diagonal stays diagonal.

Search results and review items now take you to the part of the page you asked
for, not just the page — and the highlight goes away when you move to the next
result instead of being left behind on the old one. Review shows the actual
work, and revealing an answer now changes the picture instead of doing
something you couldn't see.

"Open in Courseleaf" works. The app has been offering itself in Files, Mail and
the share sheet since the first build and then doing nothing when you tapped it.
A PDF, an image or a .courseleaf archive opened that way — or dropped onto the
library — now goes through the same import you get from the + button, asks the
same question about where to put it, and leaves the original file alone.

Known and unfinished:
• Nothing in this build has been tested on a real iPad or Apple Pencil. Pencil
  feel, lag and palm rejection are exactly what I need you to judge.
• Shapes are corrected into ink, so they erase and export like handwriting but
  cannot be re-edited later as shapes.
• Recognition covers lines, circles/ellipses and rectangles/squares only — no
  triangles or arrows yet.
• An export cannot be cancelled once it starts.
• The support and privacy pages are not published yet, and Settings says so
  rather than linking anywhere.

If something is missing or wrong, say what you did just before it — especially
anything about handwriting that did not come back.
